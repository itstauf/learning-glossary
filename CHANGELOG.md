# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are recorded here. Newest on top.

## v0.1.0 (2026-10-03)

First public release, part of The Compounding Brain series.

### Added
- `clean-transcript` skill (`.claude/skills/clean-transcript/SKILL.md`): seven phases from raw transcript to cleaned note plus glossary update.
- Glossary template with the three tiers (Watch, Suggest, Auto-apply), the five guards, five categories and an empty growth log.
- Banglish pack (Bangla-English code-switching) and English pack (tool names, jargon, fillers).
- Docs: how the ladder works, the five guards, the single-writer rule, making your own pack.
- Adapters: paste or upload (default, no connector), Granola (folder-scoped, manual trigger), and notes for Otter, Fireflies, Zoom and Teams exports.
- Worked example: one entry climbing from Watch to Auto-apply over three synthetic calls.
- Project instructions for Claude.ai and ChatGPT Projects (manual mode).
- `config/people.md` template and `config/clean-transcript.json`.
