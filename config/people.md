# People

The people source for `clean-transcript`. A name only enters the glossary's Category D (Names) if the person is listed here. This is guard 3: the glossary never invents a person.

Keep one heading per person. The heading anchor is what the glossary links to in its "People page" column, for example `config/people.md#mira-okafor`.

If you already keep people pages elsewhere (an Obsidian folder, a CRM export, a `people/` directory), point `people_path` in `config/clean-transcript.json` at that instead.
